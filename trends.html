Transportation Impact

Recent fuel price adjustments have increased transportation costs by 8-12%, particularly affecting produce from distant regions like Nuwara Eliya and Embilipitiya.

Supply Chain Optimization

New direct farmer-to-market initiatives in Dambulla and Kurunegala are reducing middleman costs, resulting in 15-20% price benefits for consumers.

Weather Alert

Heavy rainfall expected in the central highlands next week may affect vegetable supply from Nuwara Eliya region. Consider stocking up on carrots and cabbage if prices are favorable.
